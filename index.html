About IRO

Issues Reporting Outlet


IRO is a proposed web-based platform designed to provide an organized channel for reporting issues affecting AAUA students and surrounding indigenous communities.


The communities considered in this project include Akungba-Akoko, Iwaro, Etioro and Ayegunle, as well as the AAUA student community.


Project Objectives

  • Provide an accessible reporting channel.
  • Improve communication between stakeholders.
  • Allow submitted reports to be tracked.
  • Support anonymous reporting.
  • Improve documentation of community issues.
